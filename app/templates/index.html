How Skill Exchanges Work

A transparent, fair-trading cycle for college learning.

1

Post or Discover Skills

Publish what you know or request help with tough coursework. Our SymPy mathematical model automatically calculates fair point values.

2

Request & Reserve Escrow

When an instructor accepts your session, points are safely locked in escrow so you never worry about double spending or payment friction.

3

Meet, Settle & Review

Meet in the campus library or via video call. Upon completion confirmation, points transfer to the teacher and verified reviews build your reputation.

Academic Math Concept: SymPy

Transparent, Non-Arbitrary Points System

No student can artificially set arbitrary point costs. SkillSwap leverages Python's SymPy symbolic mathematics library to evaluate exact, equitable costs based on session duration, proficiency tier, and preparation effort.

Points = (Duration / 30) × BaseRate(10) × Multiplier + PrepBonus
