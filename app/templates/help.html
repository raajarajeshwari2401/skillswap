SkillSwap Platform Rules & Community Guidelines

Everything you need to know about points, escrow safety, and student mentoring.

🪙

1. How the Points Economy Works

SkillSwap is a completely cash-free campus platform. Every new student is welcomed with 50 bonus points upon registration. Points can only be earned by conducting verified peer sessions or through verified administrative grants.

Rule: You spend points when you receive instruction, and you earn points when you deliver instruction. Points cannot be arbitrarily awarded or transferred directly without a linked session.
📐

2. Automatic SymPy Mathematical Difficulty & Points Calculation

Instructors cannot inflate session prices arbitrarily. The platform server evaluates every skill offering using Python's SymPy symbolic math engine:

  • Base Rate: 10 points per 30 minutes of instruction.
  • Beginner Multiplier: 1.0x (Foundational topics, e.g. Intro Python syntax).
  • Intermediate Multiplier: 1.5x (Core coursework, e.g. Algorithms or Calculus).
  • Advanced Multiplier: 2.0x (Complex graduate/research topics, e.g. Compiler Design).
  • Preparation Effort Bonus: Up to +15 bonus points for intensive session prep.
🔒

3. Escrow Hold and Cancellation Guarantees

When an instructor accepts a session, the required points are placed in escrow reservation inside the learner's wallet. The learner cannot double-spend those points on other sessions.

If the session is cancelled before completion, the reservation is released immediately back to the learner's available balance. Points are permanently transferred to the instructor only after completion is confirmed.

🛡️

4. Campus Safety & Privacy Rules

  • Always meet in public campus areas (e.g. library quiet rooms, student centers, campus cafes) or via verified video links.
  • Never publish private home addresses or personal phone numbers in public skill descriptions.
  • If an exchange encounters any violation, click the Report Dispute button on the session page for administrator intervention.
